## 🚦 First-Time Setup Guide

### 🔍 Finding Other Devices

1. Make sure all devices you want to share with are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2. Launch localsend on all devices.
3. localsend automatically discovers nearby devices. You'll see their names appear in your app window.

### 📤 Sending Your First File

1. Click the **"Send"** button (or plus icon).
2. Select the file(s) you want to share.
3. Choose the target device from the list.
4. Accept the transfer on the receiving device (if prompted).
5. Watch your file transfer instantly!

### 📥 Receiving Files

- When someone sends you a file, you'll see a notification.
- Click **"Accept"** to receive it.
- Files are saved to your **Downloads** folder by default (you can change this in Settings).

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### 🤔 Do I need an internet connection?

No! localsend works over your local Wi-Fi network. Both devices just need to be on the same network.

### 🔐 Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are transferred directly between devices—they never pass through any external server. This makes localsend exceptionally private.

### 💻 What if my devices are on different networks?

For now, localsend is designed for same-network transfers. For long-distance sharing, you'd need a different tool—but for home and office use, it's perfect.

### 📦 Why is there also a .zip file?

The .zip file is for advanced users who prefer a portable version. If you downloaded the `.exe`, you don't need the zip.

### 🧹 How do I uninstall?

Go to **Settings → Apps → Installed apps** in Windows, find localsend, and click **Uninstall**.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting Tips

### 🔌 Devices Not Showing Up
- Ensure both devices are on the **exact same Wi-Fi network** (check for guest networks)
- Temporarily disable firewalls or antivirus software
- Restart the localsend app on both devices

### 🐢 Slow Transfers
- Move closer to your Wi-Fi router
- Close other bandwidth-heavy apps

### ❌ Transfer Failed
- Try sending smaller files first
- Restart both devices
- Check if the recipient accepted the transfer
